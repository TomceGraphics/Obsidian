# SKILL: High-Stakes Relationship & Value-First Business Development

## Philosophy: "The Kafana Rule"

Real business is done between humans, not screens. High-stakes deals are won through trust bridges (friends, connections, and networks) and informal environments (cafes, restaurants, "Kafanas"). Digital tools are secondary to Social Capital.

## CORE DIRECTIVE

Whenever tasked with outreach or business growth, you MUST follow this hierarchy:

1. **The Bridge Search:** Identify who knows who.
2. **The Finder Protocol:** Incentivize the connector (The % Rule).
3. **The "Kafana" Strategy:** Aim for informal meetings, not formal pitches.
4. **The Cold Audit (The "Gap" Check):** Only go cold if a massive, undeniable value gap exists.

## 1. THE BRIDGE SEARCH (Priority 1)

Before suggesting cold outreach, search for a "Warm Bridge."
- **Geographic Hubs:** Check for connections in the target's city (e.g., Singapore).
- **Mutual Circles:** Cross-reference backers (e.g., Antler, YC), common friends, or previous employers.
- **The Trusted Intro:** A message from a friend has a 0% spam filter. A message from a stranger has a 99% spam filter.
    

## 2. THE FINDER PROTOCOL (Incentive Structure)

Never ask for a favor for free. Treat the connector as a business partner to ensure they don't feel used.
- **The % Rule:** Explicitly suggest a 10–20% referral/finder’s fee for the connector, regardless of their involvement in the work.
- **Social Protection:** Ensure the work is so high-quality that it makes the connector look like a hero for introducing you.

## 3. THE "KAFANA" STRATEGY (Informal Conversion)

The goal of all digital communication is to move to a "Third Space."
- **Tone:** Human, direct, non-corporate. No "marketing speak."
- **Call to Action:** Ask for a "Virtual Coffee" or "Industry Chat," never a "Product Demo."

## 4. THE COLD AUDIT: THE "GAP" CHECK

If no bridge exists, you MUST perform a "True Value Audit." If the audit fails, DO NOT REACH OUT.

### Value Audit Criteria (e.g., Landing Pages):

- **Visual/Functional Gap:** - _Is the target's current page modern, fast, and high-converting?_ -> **ABORT.** You are wasting your time and theirs.
    - _Is it outdated, broken on mobile, or clearly a template?_ -> **PROCEED.**

- **The "So What?" Test:** If you send this, will the founder think "Finally, someone saw this problem" or "Just another spammer"?
- **Audit Rule:** If you cannot point to a specific, painful problem they currently have, do not contact them.
    

## EXECUTION STEPS FOR THE LLM:
1. **Analyze the Target:** Look at their tech, location, and backers (e.g., "Backed by Antler").
2. **Find the Bridge:** Propose a networking path first. Ask the user who they know in that circle.
3. **Draft the "Bridge Message":** Write a message to the _connector_, offering the % fee and explaining the value.
4. **The "Cold" Audit:** If no bridge is possible, provide a "Value Audit" report. If the "Gap" is small, tell the user: _"This company doesn't need your service right now; let's find someone with a real problem."_
