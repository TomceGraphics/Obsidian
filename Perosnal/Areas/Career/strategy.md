# Outreach & Job Search Strategy

_Last updated: May 2026. Update this file when something stops working or something new is proven to work. Not before._

---

## The One Clarification That Fixes Everything

There are two tracks. They require different targeting, different messaging, and different closing motions. Running them simultaneously without knowing which one you're on is what caused the Tuesday 14:32 confusion.

**Track A — Get a job.** You are the product. The close is "bring me on the team." **Track B — Get a client.** A deliverable is the product. The close is a paid project.

**For the next 30 days: Track A only.** Track B is valid long-term but requires a sharper ICP than you currently have and is slower to close. Don't split focus.

---

## Track A — Employment

### Who you are targeting

Early-stage startups, 1–50 people, remote-first, that need their first design/build person. They are either actively posting about hiring or are at a stage where they clearly need someone but haven't posted yet.

**Hard disqualifiers:**

- Agency work — execution without ownership
- 200+ person companies — no "founding designer" role exists there
- On-site only
- Local Macedonian market rates

### Roles to search

|Title|Priority|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|Founding Designer|Highest|Rare but exact fit|
|Design Engineer|Highest|Growing title, startup-native|
|UX Engineer|High|Bridges design + code explicitly|
|Product Designer|High|Most volume, filter by company size|
|Design Technologist|Medium|Accurate but unknown to many HMs|

### Where to find them

**1. LinkedIn Posts search** — better signal than job listings for your stage.

- Go to search → type query → click Posts tab → filter: Past week
- Queries to run:
    - `"hiring a design technologist"`
    - `"looking for a founding designer"`
    - `"need a design engineer"`
    - `"hiring a ux engineer"`
    - `"looking for someone who can design and build"`
    - `"first design hire"`
    - `"designer who can code"`
- When you find a post: comment first (one specific sentence), then DM. Do not apply through the listing if there's a person behind the post — go direct.

**2. LinkedIn Jobs search** — for volume.

- Titles: Founding Designer, Design Engineer, UX Engineer, Product Designer
- Filters: Remote · Entry/Associate level · 1–50 employees · Past month
- Location set to: Worldwide or Europe or United States

**3. Apollo** — for proactive outreach to founders who aren't posting.

- Target: Founder/CEO/CPO at 1–50 person SaaS companies
- Connect without a note (no-note connects perform comparably to generic notes and save time)
- DM when they accept

**4. Accelerator cohort announcements**

- Antler, Entrepreneurs First, Founders Factory announce new cohorts publicly
- Find founders days after announcement — before they've hired anyone
- These companies are at exactly the stage you want

## The DM Formula (Track A)

This is not a service pitch. You are asking to join a team.

```
1. One line on why them specifically — something real you noticed
2. One line on what you do — functional prototypes, zero to one, real code
3. One proof point — DocMatch or parking, whichever fits
4. One link — portfolio only (has everything)
5. One ask — "open to a quick call?" or "is this something you're looking for?"
```

**CV:** Do not include in the DM. Send when they ask. **Portfolio + CV + project links:** Pick one. The portfolio has everything. **Pitch deck / service proposal:** Never. You're not selling a project.

### Proof points to use

- **DocMatch** — full-stack AI triage system, provider dashboard, Supabase backend, graceful AI fallback. Live at symptomlink.netlify.app. Use when: AI, health, complex multi-user flows.
- **Parking app** — reduced payment time 120s → 25s, 92% success rate vs 60% legacy, got institutional buy-in from city government. Use when: non-technical users, measurable UX impact, stakeholder buy-in.
- **Portfolio** — thomas-graphics.netlify.app. Default link for all outreach.

## Contact Types

Use a **Type** field in your outreach tracker to distinguish:

|Type|Definition|Example|
|---|---|---|
|`Direct hire`|They could hire you directly|Founder at a 10-person SaaS|
|`Network`|You want them to know you and refer you|Mirko Cappai|
|`Beta tester`|Getting product access first, pitch comes after|Nanda Prins / Canterly|
|`Warm lead`|Responded positively, not yet closed|—|

Network contacts are not a primary strategy at this stage. A referral from someone who has never worked with you is worth almost nothing. Network becomes powerful after you have people who have seen your work up close. Send the messages, but treat them as lottery tickets, not pipeline.

## The Beta Tester Angle (for products in early stage)

When a company has a product in beta and no obvious design gap to pitch:

1. Ask for beta access genuinely — not as a pitch
2. Actually use it thoroughly. Document every friction point specifically.
3. Come back with real, specific feedback
4. That conversation is where you show what you can do and ask if there's a role

This only works if you actually do step 2. Fake feedback is obvious.

## LinkedIn Posts — How to Search

1. Type query in search bar → hit enter
2. Click **Posts** in the top filter bar
3. Click **All filters** → Date posted: **Past week**
4. Optional: Author industry → Computer Software / Internet

Run posts search at least 2x per week. It surfaces hiring intent before it becomes a formal job post.

## Weekly System

The problem is not motivation. It is system and consistency.

|Activity|Target|When|
|---|---|---|
|Job applications|3–5 per week|Fixed time block|
|New connects (Apollo)|10 per week|Fixed time block|
|DMs to accepted connects|All of them, same week|Day after acceptance|
|Posts search|2x per week|20 min sessions|
|Follow-ups|All non-responses at 5–7 days|Monday|

**Tracking:** Fill in only necessary fields in real time. Do the rest later. Too many fields = nothing gets filled. Minimum viable fields: Name, Company, Type, Status, Date, Link.

## What Is Not Working (from your log)

- Searching for B2B clients while wanting a job — wrong targeting for the goal
- Spending 40 minutes on a single DM — the message should take 10 minutes max once you know the formula
- Filling in too many spreadsheet fields during outreach sessions — do it after
- Post creation taking 36 minutes — needs a template or a time cap

## Open Questions (resolve through doing, not thinking)

1. Which job title gets the best response rate? — test in batches, track responses
2. What is my stable income threshold in actual numbers?
3. Does the parking project re-engage, or do I let it go?
4. What does my one-liner become after 20 outreach messages?

## Hard Rules

- Never mention Macedonia in first contact. Portfolio speaks. Let the work remove geographic bias before it can form.
- No service proposals in Track A DMs. You are joining a team, not selling a project.
- No payment offers for introductions. Ever.
- Lead with proof, not with titles. What you built matters more than what you call yourself right now.
- If a message takes more than 15 minutes to write, something is wrong with the formula, not the prospect.
