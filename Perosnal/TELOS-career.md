## WHO I AM

I am a 22-year-old Design Technologist based in Ohrid, North Macedonia. I sit at the intersection of UX thinking, frontend engineering, and AI-directed product development and design.

I don't draw interfaces — I build functional prototypes that prove a UX actually works. My process skips static mockups and goes straight to working code: real data, real interactions, real user behavior.

I grew up watching inefficient systems be accepted as normal. I refuse to accept that. I am not yet financially independent, but I am building toward it — deliberately and without shortcuts.

---

## THE PROBLEM I CARE ABOUT

Most digital products fail their users not because of bad aesthetics, but because no one validated whether the UX actually works before it shipped. Design is treated as how something looks. I believe design is how something _behaves_ — and that can only be proven in a functional prototype, not a Figma frame.

Separately: talented people in small or economically limited markets (like Macedonia) are priced and perceived below their actual global market value. Geography should not determine what your work is worth.

### CASE STUDIES (shipped, documented, with HCI reasoning)

###### POF1: DocMatch (SymptomLink)
AI-driven medical triage system. Patients describe symptoms in natural language; the system routes them to the correct specialist.
- Stack: Vanilla JS, Tailwind, Supabase, Gemini 2.5 Flash
- Features: Provider dashboard, patient records, booking system, graceful AI fallback
- Key insight: Shifting the "translation burden" from human to machine significantly lowers friction. Graceful AI degradation via local keyword fallback.
- Status: Live at symptomlink.netlify.app
- What it proves: Can architect and ship a full-stack product with real AI integration

###### POF2: Parking Solution — City of Ohrid
Built and pitched a digital parking management system to the city. Pitch accepted.
- Role: Product Designer & Full Stack Developer
- Stack: Figma, React, Tailwind CSS
- Key metrics: Payment time reduced from 120s to 25s. 92% payment success rate vs 60% legacy. NPS: +64.
- Key insight: Pitching "revenue recovery" secured institutional buy-in immediately. Removing 40% of planned features increased success rates.
- Status: Pitch accepted. Project on hold — director unavailable, delay is theirs not mine.
- What it proves: Can take a real-world problem, build a solution, and get institutional buy-in

###### POF3: Bank Log — Adaptive NLI Interface
"Notepad-First" PWA for personal finance tracking using natural language input.
- Role: HCI Researcher & Lead Developer
- Stack: Vanilla JS, IndexedDB (PWA), Regex Engine, Tailwind CSS, ApexCharts
- Design principle: "The system should adapt to human input format. The user should never be asked to think like a database."
- Key insight: Shifting translation work from human to background processor reduced data entry friction by 60%. Handles "30300", "30.3k", "30,300" seamlessly.
- What it proves: Deep HCI thinking applied to a mundane problem, shipped as a real PWA
