# Competitive Forces Model for IT infrastructure investment

Adapts Porter's Five Forces to help firms decide *how much* and *where* to invest in IT infrastructure.

## The six forces (Figure 5.13)
1. **Market demand** — what capabilities do customers and markets expect?
2. **Business strategy** — what does the firm's overall strategy require from IT?
3. **IT strategy, infrastructure, and cost** — what can the firm afford and sustain?
4. **IT assessment** — what is the current state of IT maturity?
5. **Competitor IT services** — what are competitors offering via their IT?
6. **Competitor IT infrastructure investments** — what are competitors spending on?

## How to use it
A firm analyzes all six forces to determine the right level of IT investment — not too little (falling behind competitors) and not too much (wasting capital).
