# Data governance

The set of **policies, processes, roles, and standards** that ensure data across an organization is accurate, consistent, secure, and used responsibly.

## Why it's needed
As organizations accumulate more data, questions arise:
- Who owns this data?
- Who can access it?
- How long should it be kept?
- What happens when data is wrong or inconsistent across systems?

## Key components
- **Data stewardship** — designated roles responsible for data quality in each domain
- **Data policies** — rules for how data is collected, stored, shared, and deleted
- **Metadata management** — documentation of what data means and where it comes from
- **Compliance** — alignment with regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.)

## Relationship to data quality
Governance is the *framework*. [[Data Quality Assurance]] is the *practice* that operates within that framework.
