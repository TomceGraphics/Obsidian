# Management and governance (IT)

How organizations make decisions about IT infrastructure and ensure those decisions align with business goals.

## IT governance defined
A framework of policies, processes, and roles that ensures IT investments support business objectives and manage risk appropriately.

## Key decisions IT governance covers
- What infrastructure to invest in
- Who owns technology decisions (IT dept vs business units)
- How to handle security, compliance, and data policies
- How to measure IT performance (SLAs)

## Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
Formal contracts between IT providers and business users specifying:
- Uptime guarantees (e.g. 99.9%)
- Response times
- Penalties for non-compliance
