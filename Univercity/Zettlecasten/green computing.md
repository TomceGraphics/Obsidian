# Green computing

Reducing the environmental impact of IT infrastructure.

## Core idea
IT consumes significant energy — data centers alone account for ~1-2% of global electricity use. Green computing aims to minimize this footprint.

## Strategies
- **Virtualization** — run multiple virtual machines on one physical server, reducing hardware needed
- **Cloud computing** — shared infrastructure is more energy-efficient than dedicated servers per firm
- **Energy-efficient hardware** — processors and storage designed for lower power draw
- **Data center design** — cooling optimization, renewable energy sourcing

## Why firms care
- Cost: lower energy bills reduce TCO ([[TCO]])
- Regulation: increasing legal pressure (esp. in EU)
- Reputation: sustainability as a competitive differentiator
