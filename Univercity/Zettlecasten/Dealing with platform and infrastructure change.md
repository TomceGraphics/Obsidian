# Dealing with platform and infrastructure change

Managing the transition when a firm upgrades or replaces its IT infrastructure.

## Why it's hard
- Legacy systems are deeply integrated with business processes
- Staff are trained on existing platforms
- Migration carries risk of downtime or data loss
- Costs are unpredictable

## Common strategies
- **Phased migration** — move gradually rather than all at once
- **Parallel running** — run old and new systems simultaneously during transition
- **Cloud migration** — moving from on-premise to cloud reduces hardware change complexity
- **Vendor lock-in awareness** — switching costs must be factored into long-term planning

## Governance role
IT governance (→ [[Management and governance]]) is essential during transitions to ensure decisions are coordinated and risks are managed.
