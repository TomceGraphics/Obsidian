*the key terms I will cover here are Upstream and Downstream*

### Upstream vs. Downstream
- **Upstream:** This includes the company's **suppliers** and the suppliers' suppliers. It represents the network that flows _into_ the company. If you manufacture smartphones, your upstream network includes the mining companies providing lithium, the chip manufacturers, and the factories assembling the components.

- **Downstream:** This includes the organizations and processes responsible for distributing and delivering the finished products to the **final customers**. For that same smartphone company, downstream activities include shipping logistics, wholesalers, retail stores, and online fulfillment centers.

### Information vs. Material Flow
While materials usually flow downstream (from supplier to customer), information flows in both directions.
- **Downstream flow:** Delivery schedules, shipping alerts, and product instructions.
- **Upstream flow:** Orders, payment information, and returns.
