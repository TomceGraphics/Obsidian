questions:
how can packet switching be used for downloading a file?
is cdn in ism the same as cdn in web development?

[^1]: packet switching is the process of breaking down data into smaller packets and sending them across a network
[^2]: CDN is a network of servers that are distributed geographically and are used to deliver content to users based on their location
[^3]: TCP/IP is the protocol suite that governs how data is transmitted over the internet it has 4 layers (application, transport, internet, network interface). 
[^4]: IP addressing is the process of assigning an IP address to a device that is connected to a network. 
[^5]: DNS is the process of converting a domain name to an IP address. 
[^6]: ipv6 is the latest version of the IP addressing protocol it has 128-bit addresses
[^7]: internet2 is a high-speed internet backbone network that connects universities and research institutions
[^8]: FTP is the process of transferring files between computers
[^9]: VoIP is a protocol that enables the transmission of voice over the internet. it is the process of making phone calls over the internet
[^10]: VPN is the process of creating a secure connection over a public network
[^11]: unified communications is the process of integrating different communication methods
[^12]: OSS is the process of managing the operations of a telecommunications network
[^13]: BSS is the process of managing the business operations of a telecommunications network

Q: How do enterprise systems help businesses achieve operational excellence?
A:

[^14]: upstream supply chain is the part of the supply chain that deals with the flow of raw materials from suppliers to manufacturers
[^15]: downstream supply chain is the part of the supply chain that deals with the flow of finished goods from manufacturers to customers
[^16]: lean manufacturing is a manufacturing process that focuses on minimizing waste and maximizing efficiency
[^17]: JIT is the process of having raw materials delivered to a manufacturing facility only when they are needed. This reduces the need for storage space and minimizes waste.
[^18]: bullwhip effect is a phenomenon in supply chain management where demand variability increases as you move up the supply chain
[^19]: push based manufacturing is when the products are made in anticipation of customer demand. pull based manufacturing is when the products are made in response to customer demand
