## 📺 Top YouTube "Ambience" Channels (Visuals + Audio)
These channels are legendary for mixing game music with 4K loops of rain, snow, or coffee shop vibes.

1.  **[Ambient Worlds](https://youtube.com)** - High-quality mixes from *Lord of the Rings*, *Harry Potter*, and *Skyrim*.
2.  **[Vibe](https://youtube.com)** - Focuses heavily on *Minecraft* and *Lofi* aesthetics.
3.  **[Everness](https://youtube.com)** - The go-to for *The Elder Scrolls* and *Witcher* world ambiences (nature sounds + music).
4.  **[The ASMR Geek](https://youtube.com)** - Excellent for "room" ambiences (e.g., studying in a Slytherin common room or a space station).
